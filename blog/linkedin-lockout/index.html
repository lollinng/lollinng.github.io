I rate-limited myself out of LinkedIn

1.What I was building

I'm job hunting, and I automate anything I do more than twice. My pipeline finds job posts, reads them, scores them against my profile and lines up the good ones for applying. Most of it reads job boards that publish their listings on purpose. The LinkedIn part was different: it drove a real, signed-in browser.

Two kinds of work went through it:

  • Search: open a results page and click through the cards. Every card you click is a new URL, so one search is dozens of page loads.
  • Detail read (I called it "validating" a lead): open one saved job to get its full description and where its Apply button goes.

One lead, before and after a detail read (dummy)


  


  
Every page load spent from a rolling budget, and a circuit breaker stopped everything at the first login wall or security check. Simplified:


# per 15 minutes, per hour, per day
LIMITS = {"search": (3, 6, 12), "detail": (6, 12, 24)}

def can_open(kind, log, now):
    per_15, per_hour, per_day = LIMITS[kind]
    return (log.count(kind, since=now - 15 * MIN) < per_15
        and log.count(kind, since=now - HOUR) < per_hour
        and log.count(kind, since=now - DAY) < per_day)

if page.looks_like_checkpoint():
    breaker.trip("login_or_challenge")   # nothing runs until a human says so

  
The breaker worked perfectly. It just fired after the damage was done.

5.The ID check that can't read my ID

  
Getting a restricted account back means proving who you are through Persona, LinkedIn's identity-verification partner. Persona never accepted any of my Indian IDs. LinkedIn's own help page says why: "Our verification system does not currently support the country that issued your identification document." India does have a DigiLocker option, but it's for the profile badge in the mobile app, not for unlocking an account.

I appealed, then wrote to support through LinkedIn's Help Center form. Gmail has the rest, times in IST:

Me17:19

    
"the link you have provided again opens a persona for me which fails in India. None of my documents works with persona for me"

  
LinkedIn Member Safety and Recovery17:33

    
"Your account has violated the LinkedIn User Agreement and Professional Community Policies. Due to the number and/or the severity of these violations, your account will remain restricted."

  
Seventeen minutes from "please verify your ID" to "will remain restricted". Fair enough. LinkedIn's User Agreement forbids scraping and automated access, and it allows one account per person. I broke both rules, and the rules won.
