Chat assistant privacy notice

Last updated 11 November 2026. Back to the site

Who you are talking to

The chat window on this site is an AI assistant, not a person. It answers from what is published on this site about my work and services. I, Prathamesh Jadhav, read the conversations and follow up myself when you ask for that.

What it collects

  • Your messages and the assistant's replies, with the time of each.
  • Details you choose to give, such as your name, email, what you want built and your timeline.
  • A random chat ID kept in your browser, so the conversation is still there when you come back. No cookies, and no advertising or tracking scripts.
  • Your IP address is used to limit abuse. It is not stored with the conversation; it appears in the hosting provider's request logs, which are kept for 30 days.

What it is used for

To answer your questions, and to reply to your enquiry if you leave contact details. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing, and you are contacted only about your enquiry.

Where it goes

  • The assistant runs on Google Cloud. Your messages are processed by Google's Gemini model through Vertex AI to write the replies.
  • Conversations and enquiries are stored in a Postgres database hosted by Neon, in Singapore.

How long it is kept

Conversations and enquiries are deleted 12 months after the last message.

Your choices

You can use this site without the chat. To see, correct or delete what the assistant holds about you, email contact@prathameshjadhav.in.
